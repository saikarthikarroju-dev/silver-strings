Tarkasi: A Legacy of Light

Born from the royal courts of the Nizams and the skilled hands of the Vishwakarma community, Karimnagar's Silver Filigree is a dance of precision.

Known as Tarkasi, this rare art form transforms pure silver into an intricate web of zig-zag patterns and floral motifs, each piece a testament to patience and heritage.
